Contact

Email support@menuswap.app for account help, bug reports, privacy questions, or data requests.

Email support

What to include

  • The email on your MenuSwap account (if you have one)
  • Your phone type (iPhone / Android) and app version if you know it
  • What you tried and what went wrong
  • Screenshots if useful

Common help

  • Delete account: Profile → Delete account (removes synced cloud data when signed in with a real account).
  • Demo login: The demo account stays on this device only and does not sync to the cloud.
  • Privacy: See our Privacy Policy.

Response time

We aim to reply within a few business days. MenuSwap is an early product — thanks for your patience.
